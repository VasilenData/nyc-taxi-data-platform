Coverage and exclusion rules

Pickups use the source’s NYC local civil time. Source-month duplicates are quarantined by trip identity. Invalid timestamps, durations, distances, fares, zones and nonfinite values are excluded; the first failing rule is recorded. Unknown and EWR lookup categories are retained when valid.

Recorded total amount is sum(total_amount), including recorded fare, taxes, tolls, tips and other components. It is not net earnings. This explorer does not filter the separate weekday/hour mart.
